Welcome to Tic Tac Toe!

You will be playing Tic Tac Toe against a computer that will make a move when you click any square.
Squares that you click during your turn will be marked with an X, squares the computer clicks will be marked with an O.
In order to win, you must get 3 Xs in a row before the computer does.
